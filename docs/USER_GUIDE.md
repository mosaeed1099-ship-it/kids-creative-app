# User Guide

Kids Creative Studio is a safe, offline creative playground for children. There
are no accounts, no ads, and nothing is uploaded anywhere — everything your
child makes stays on the device. This guide walks through what each part does.

## Getting started

Open the app in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox). If it was
given to you as a folder, you can also open it from a small local server (your
administrator can set this up). Once it loads, you'll see the home screen with
the creative activities. No sign-in is needed, and it keeps working even with
the Internet turned off.

The whole interface is in Arabic and reads right-to-left, designed for little
hands with large, friendly buttons.

## The activities

**Coloring.** Your child picks a picture and fills it in by tapping colors and
then tapping areas of the drawing. Finished pages can be saved and printed. Work
in progress is remembered, so a half-colored page is still there next time.

**Coloring Library.** A browsable gallery of every coloring page, with search,
categories, favorites, and a "recently opened" list to jump back in quickly.

**Trace Studio.** Guided tracing over letters and shapes to practice fine motor
control and handwriting, with dotted guides to follow.

**Creative Studio.** A build-a-character playground: your child arranges movable
parts (heads, ears, tails, and more), swaps expressions, and poses characters on
the canvas.

**Learning Activities.** Nine kinds of educational games — connect the dots,
mazes, match the shadow, match the shape, spot the difference, complete the
pattern, number games, letter games, and picture puzzles. Each awards up to
three stars, gives gentle hints, and unlocks reward badges as your child
progresses. Activities can be filtered by difficulty and age.

**Print & PDF Center.** Parents can preview, organize, and print any printable
page, build a print queue, and download activity sheets as a PDF or a ZIP — all
generated on the device, offline.

**Parent Dashboard.** A private space for grown-ups to create child profiles and
see progress and achievements.

## Stars, hints, and rewards

In the Learning Activities, finishing cleanly earns more stars; using hints or
making mistakes earns fewer, so there's always a reason to try again. New badges
appear on the celebration screen as milestones are reached. Everything is stored
locally on the device.

## Printing

From the Print & PDF Center, choose a page, adjust the settings (paper size,
orientation, margins, color or black-and-white), and print — or download a PDF or
ZIP to print later. Nothing leaves the device.

## Privacy & safety

There are no accounts, no chat, no ads, and no data collection. Progress and
saved artwork live only in this browser on this device (in local storage). To
clear everything, a grown-up can use the browser's "clear site data" option, or
the reset controls inside the Parent Dashboard / activities.

## Tips

- The app works fully offline — great for travel or screen-time without Wi‑Fi.
- If a page ever looks stuck, refreshing the browser is safe; saved work and
  progress are preserved.
- For the best experience on a tablet, add it to the home screen (see the
  [Administrator Guide](ADMIN_GUIDE.md)) so it opens full-screen like an app.
